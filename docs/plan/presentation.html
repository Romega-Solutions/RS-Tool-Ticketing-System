One tool, three wins

☰

Your Tasks

Kanban board + list view for all 4 projects (C1–C4). Assign, prioritize, and track — all in one place.

📄

Your Reports

Weekly report auto-generates from your tasks. Just fill in Client Engagement, Risks, and Ideas — the rest writes itself.

📊

Your Dashboard

See all projects, deadlines, and blockers on one screen. Know who's working on what without asking.

From 60 minutes to 10

Before

  • Under 10 minutes

Familiar tools, better workflow

☐

Kanban Board

Drag tasks between Backlog, To Do, In Progress, In Review, and Done. Just like Trello, but connected to your reports.

◉

Dashboard

Project cards for C1–C4, upcoming deadlines, team workload bars, and active blockers — all on one screen.

✎

Report Editor

7 sections, same as the Excel template. Sections 4 and 5 auto-fill. Auto-saves every 30 seconds — no more lost work.

📥

Excel Export

One click to export as .xlsx. Same format, same sections, same layout management is used to.
