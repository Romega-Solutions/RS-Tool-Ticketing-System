## Slide 2: The Problem We All Feel

**Heading:** Sound familiar?

**3 pain points (icon + text each):**

1. **"It's Friday again..."**
   Your weekly report takes 30-60 minutes — re-typing things you already tracked in Trello or your TODO list.

2. **"Where is that task?"**
   Tasks are scattered across markdown files, Trello boards, Google Sheets, and chat messages. No single place to check.

3. **"Who's doing what?"**
   No way to see team workload, blockers, or project status at a glance. You have to ask around or dig through files.

## Slide 4: How Weekly Reports Change

**Heading:** From 60 minutes to 10

**Before (left side — red/negative):**
- Open Excel template
- Remember what you did this week
- Re-type every task, project, accomplishment
- Fill all 7 sections manually
- 30-60 minutes every Friday

**After (right side — green/positive):**
- Open your report — 2 sections already filled
- Pending Projects = pulled from your active tasks
- Key Accomplishments = pulled from tasks you completed
- You only type: Client Engagement, Risks, Ideas
- Under 10 minutes

**Bottom note:** Reports still export to the same Excel format management expects.

## Slide 5: What It Looks Like

**Heading:** Familiar tools, better workflow

**4 feature callouts with descriptions:**

1. **Kanban Board** — Drag tasks between Backlog → To Do → In Progress → In Review → Done. Just like Trello, but connected to your reports.

2. **Dashboard** — Project cards for C1-C4, your upcoming deadlines, team workload bars, and active blockers — all on one screen.

3. **Report Editor** — 7 sections, same as the Excel template. Sections 4 and 5 auto-fill. Auto-saves every 30 seconds — no more lost work.

4. **Excel Export** — One click to export your report as .xlsx. Same format, same sections, same layout management is used to.
